1. Core Privacy Philosophy: Defense-Grade Air-Gapped Privacy

PCM Smart PDF was architected from inception on a fundamental premise: Your confidential documents belong exclusively to you and must never touch third-party servers. Unlike traditional cloud converters and web-based PDF editors that upload your private files across the internet, PCM Smart PDF executes 100% of its computational operations entirely on your local device hardware.

2. Apple App Store Privacy Nutrition Labels: Zero Data Collection

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.1) and Google Play Data Safety requirements:

  • Data Collected: ZERO (0). We do not collect, harvest, log, or transmit any user data.
  • Data Linked to You: NONE. No personal accounts, emails, or user profiles are required.
  • Third-Party Tracking: NONE. The application contains zero tracking frameworks, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry analytics.
  • Apple Privacy Manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`): Formally declares `NSPrivacyTracking: false` and empty collected data arrays.

3. Device Permissions & Usage Explanations

PCM Smart PDF only requests hardware permissions when strictly necessary for local functionality:

  • Camera (`NSCameraUsageDescription`): Used exclusively on your local device to scan physical paper documents into PDFs. Camera frames are processed strictly in device memory and never transmitted.
  • Microphone (`NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`): Used solely for hands-free local speech recognition commands (e.g. saying "Capture" or "Scan"). Voice audio is processed locally and never recorded or uploaded.
  • Photo Library (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`): Used only when you intentionally pick an existing document or handwritten signature image from your gallery.

4. Document Processing & Storage

All document reading, form filling, OCR text extraction, multi-language translation, digital signature stamping, and 3-tier compression take place inside sandboxed local device memory. Output documents are saved directly to your local file storage or Downloads folder.

5. Security of Personal Data

Because no data is ever transmitted to or stored on external servers, there is zero risk of server-side data leaks, database breaches, or unauthorized surveillance.

6. Developer Contact & Intellectual Property

PCM Smart PDF is designed, developed, and owned by PRAKASH CHAND MEHTA. If you have any inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer directly.

7. Account Profile Data & Justification Matrix

While document processing remains 100% on-device air-gapped, initial account registration captures the following strictly necessary licensing fields:

  • Full Legal Name: Cryptographically embedded into your digital license certificate and legal document watermarks for verifiable notarization.
  • Email Address: Used for offline activation tokens, passwordless MFA security, and mandatory statutory renewal notices.
  • Mobile Number: Used for multi-factor security alerts, license breach warnings, and account recovery.
  • Physical Address: Required for jurisdiction tax reporting (GST / VAT / Sales Tax) and fiscal invoicing compliance.

Under GDPR, CCPA, UAE PDPL, and India DPDP Act, users may view, rectify, export (JSON), or permanently erase their account profile directly inside the application settings.

8. Strict Zero Raw Card Data Billing Guarantee

PCM Smart PDF adheres to strict PCI-DSS standards: We never collect, process, or store raw credit card numbers or CVV security codes. All paid individual, business, and corporate transactions are handled exclusively via secure tokenized gateways (Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay) or direct corporate wire invoicing.
